# Fonts

All five families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows
bundling and redistribution with software. Latin subsets, downloaded once from
Google Fonts at build time and served from this directory, so the app never
requests a font over the network.

- Inter — Rasmus Andersson
- Archivo — Omnibus-Type
- Instrument Serif — Instrument
- IBM Plex Mono — IBM
- Syne — Bonjour Monde
